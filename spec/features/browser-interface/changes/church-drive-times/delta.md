# Church drive times in results

## ADDED

- AC-110: Results expose `Drive to Church (min)` as a numeric, sortable, filterable column beside
  price, visible in the Deciding view. Existing saved arrangements retain their choices and receive
  the new column by the normal new-column placement behavior. Ascending and descending sorting
  compare numbers, with blanks last. Result and property pages identify Old Zion Hill Baptist
  Church, its address, the house-to-church direction, estimated driving without live traffic, and
  openrouteservice by HeiGIT / OpenStreetMap attribution. Property detail shows the saved minutes,
  status, fetched date and normal fresh/stale/missing states. No page read makes a routing request.
