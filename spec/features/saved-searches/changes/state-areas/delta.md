## MODIFIED

- **AC-2: area forms**
  - Was: Areas support polygon, city, county, ZIP code, and radius forms, and a named polygon keeps
    its name.
  - Now: Areas support polygon, city, county, ZIP code, state, and radius forms, and a named polygon
    keeps its name. A state may be written by its name or its two-letter code, and the two are one
    state.

- **Vocabulary: area**
  - Was: An area is one geographic component of a search: a polygon, a city, a county, a ZIP code,
    or a radius around a point.
  - Now: An area is one geographic component of a search: a polygon, a city, a county, a ZIP code, a
    state, or a radius around a point.
